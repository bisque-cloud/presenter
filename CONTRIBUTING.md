# Contributing

Thanks for wanting to make `/present` better.

- **Found a bug?** [Open an issue](https://github.com/bisque-cloud/present/issues) with what you ran, what happened, and your
  OS + agent (Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Codex). If narration sounded wrong,
  the sentence that misbehaved is the single most useful thing you can give
  us.
- **Have a fix or improvement?** Open a pull request — small and focused
  beats large and sweeping. For anything substantial, an issue first saves
  you from building something we can't take.

We review every PR. Changes reach this repo through our release process, so
your PR may be closed with your change committed and credited rather than merged in
place — either way, you'll see your change (and your name on it) in the repo
and in the next release.
